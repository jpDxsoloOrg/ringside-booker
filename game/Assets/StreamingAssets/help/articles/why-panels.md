Every number the game works out can tell you why it is what it is. That's the Why? panel.

## Opening one

Hover a number, or move to it with `Tab`, and a small **?** appears next to it. Click it, or press `Enter`, and the panel opens on the right.

You'll find them on cash, popularity, morale, expected stars, show ratings, crowds, contract asks, every ledger payment and more.

## Reading it

The final value sits at the top. Below it are the steps that built it. Groups open and close, and **Expand all** opens everything.

Some steps name a person or a promotion. Click the name to go to them.

**Copy as text** copies the whole breakdown, which is handy for notes or a bug report.

Close it with the cross, `Escape` or a click outside.

## Older numbers

The full breakdown is kept for recent results. For older ones the panel shows what moved the number most, and for other promotions' results it keeps only a summary.

> **Tip:** When something surprises you, open its Why?. I built the game so that every number can explain itself, and nothing is hidden from you.
