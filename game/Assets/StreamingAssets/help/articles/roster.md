The Roster is everyone who works for you: wrestlers, managers, announcers, referees, producers and the rest. Each group has its own tab.

## Cards or table

The roster starts as cards, one per person. Switch to **Table** for a list you can sort.

In the table, click a header to sort by it. The columns button at the end of the header adds or hides extra columns, such as momentum, style and age. Click a row to open it and see their skills, what they expect and the traits worth knowing.

Use the search box and **Filter** to narrow it down by side, division or champions.

## Worth a look

The strip at the top points out what to check: who is hurting, who is unhappy, whose contracts end soon and who expects a bigger push.

## Setting a push

A push is where someone sits on the card, from **Main eventer** down to **Enhancement**. Use **Set push** on a wrestler to change it.

> **Note:** Pushing someone below where they expect to be costs morale. Pushing them above it raises what they expect next time.

## Injuries

The **Injuries** tab lists who is out, how bad it is and when they're back. Opening it marks new injuries as seen, which lets the day move again.

## A closer look

Click anyone to open a quick look with their popularity, skills and contract. **Full profile** has their career, health and form.

Popularity, morale and the other numbers have a quiet [Why?](help:why-panels) next to them.
