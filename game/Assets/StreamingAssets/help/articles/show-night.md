On show night your card goes live. You run it one segment at a time, then read how it went.

## Running the show

Press **Start the show**, then **Run the next segment** for each one. The live bar shows where you are and how the show is doing so far.

If you'd rather not watch, **Sim the rest** runs everything that's left at once. The results are the same either way.

**Leave and finish later** takes you back to the Office. The show waits for you.

## Keys

- `Space` runs the next segment.
- `S` sims the rest.

## If it won't start

The show can't start when nothing is booked, when it isn't on tonight or when the card has problems. Each message has **Open the card**, which takes you to [Booking](help:booking#the-card-check).

## The results

When the show is over, **See the results** opens the night's report.

- The show's rating, as stars and a word from _Awful_ up to _Classic_.
- **Attendance**, **Gate**, **Viewers** and the change in your popularity.
- Every segment as a card. Click one for its full report: result, rating, why it rated so and what it cost the people in it.
- **Key changes**: new champions, injuries, popularity and money.
- **Star of the night**.

> **Tip:** The rating, the crowd and the popularity change all have a [Why?](help:why-panels). It's the quickest way to learn what makes a good show.
