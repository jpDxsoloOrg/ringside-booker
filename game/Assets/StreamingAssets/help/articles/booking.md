Booking is where you build each show's card: who wrestles whom, who talks, and in what order.

## Pick a show

The strip at the top lists your next shows. The ones that still need booking stand out. **Calendar** shows the rest.

The header shows the show's size, from house show to flagship, when it airs, and its venue. **Choose venue** lets you pick a building, or **Pick for me** picks one.

## Slots

A show is a row of slots in running order. Each slot has a role, such as the main event, and a length: **Short**, **Standard** or **Long**.

The **Time used** bar shows how much of the show's time the card takes. A longer match raises its star limit and what the crowd expects, so give the time to the matches that deserve it. If the bar runs over, shorten a match or drop a segment.

## Fill the card

- **Auto-fill** books every empty slot the way the computer would. It keeps what you've already booked.
- **Add a match** or **Add a talking segment** fills one slot yourself.
- Drag a slot's handle to move it. Drop it on another booked slot to swap them.
- **Clear** empties the whole card, after asking first.

Click a slot to see its details on the right: the expected stars, the winner, the length and what's on the line. **More options** sets the result, the storylines and the running order.

> **Tip:** Start with **Auto-fill**, then change the main event and anything else you care about.

## The card check

The pill under the header tells you if the card is ready. Click it for the list. Each problem has a **Fix** button that takes you to the slot.

## Show night

When the day comes, the day button takes you to the show. See [Show night](help:show-night).
