Finances answers two questions: am I making money, and how long will the cash last?

## Overview

The banner at the top gives the verdict, such as _You're making money_ or _Cash is running out_, and how many months your cash covers at this rate.

Below it:

- **Cash**, the months of cash left, this month so far and TV income.
- **This month by category**: money in and money out. Click a line to see those payments.
- **What you pay every month**: wages, medical and production. Wages are paid every day.
- **Last 12 months**: open it to see each month's money in, money out and closing cash.

## Ledger

The **Ledger** lists every payment, newest first. Filter it by month, money in or money out. Every amount has a [Why?](help:why-panels) that shows how it was worked out.

## Policies

**Policies** sets how much you spend on travel, catering and medical care. Each level shows what it costs a month and what it changes. A change applies from the next show or the next 1st of the month.

## Running out

When cash goes below zero, costs are still paid, so the debt grows every month. Cut costs, sign cheaper talent or put on bigger shows to climb back.

> **Tip:** Shows bring the money in. Ticket sales, merchandise and TV all come from putting on shows people want to see.
