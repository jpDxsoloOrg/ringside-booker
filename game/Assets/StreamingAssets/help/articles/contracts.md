Contracts is where you sign people, keep them and let them go.

## The tabs

- **Active**: everyone under contract.
- **Expiring**: contracts that end soon.
- **Wants to leave**: people who asked to be released.
- **Ended**: contracts that are over, and how they ended.
- **Free agents**: people you can sign.
- **Offers**: your talks, open and closed.
- **Trades**: trades with rival promotions: open, answered and the history.

Cards at the top flag what needs your decision. The sidebar's red badge counts the people who want to leave.

## Signing someone

1. Open **Free agents** and pick someone. The **Ask** column shows what they want.
2. Press **Make offer**. The sheet shows their ask, and **Use their ask** copies it into your offer.
3. Change the wage, length, card position or the terms under **More terms**. The meter shows how they'd take it as you go.
4. Press **Submit offer**. They answer after the next day.

They might sign, turn you down or come back with a counter-offer. You can accept the counter, send a revised offer or end the talks.

> **Note:** If talks end badly, they won't talk to you again for a while. The table shows until when.

## Renewing

**Renew** works in the last stretch before a contract ends. Before that, the button is greyed out and says when talks open.

## A different job

You can offer someone on your roster a different job at any time: a manager can get a shot in the ring, or a wrestler can move to managing or commentary. Open their contract and press **Offer a different job** (in renewal talks, it sits at the top of your offer). Pick the job, and you'll see their answer straight away. Hover **Why?** to see what it's based on: how good they'd be at it, their age and health, how happy they are, and the pay and spotlight. They give their final answer after the next day.

## Trades

You can swap workers and cash with a promotion the computer runs. Press **Propose a trade** on Trades or on a rival's page, or **Offer in a trade** on any worker's profile or quick look (yours or a rival's).

1. Pick the rival, then add workers to **You give** and **You get** (up to five a side), and cash on one side if you like.
2. The meter shows how they'd take it as you go (**Likely yes**, **Close** or **Far apart**), and each worker says whether they're happy to move. Hover **Why?** for the reasons.
3. **More terms** on a worker lets you change their pay or length. A pay rise can win over someone who doesn't want to go.
4. Press **Send offer**. They answer after the next day: yes, no, or a counter-offer you can **Accept**, **Refuse** or **Change and send back**.

Champions can't be traded: drop or vacate the title first. Some top names have a no-trade clause and only go if they want to. Rivals sometimes offer you a trade too; it waits a week, then lapses.

## Letting people go

Open a contract and press **Release**. You pay severance, and the sheet shows how much and what's left in the bank.

When someone asks to leave, you choose: **Let them go** now with severance, or **Keep them**. Keep an unhappy worker too long and they might walk out.
