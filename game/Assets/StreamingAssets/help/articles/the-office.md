The Office is your home screen. It shows what's coming up, what changed and what needs you, all on one page.

## The top bar

The top bar is on every screen.

- **Date** shows today and the season. Hover it for the full date.
- **Cash** is the money in the bank. Below zero it says _In debt_.
- **Popularity** is how well known you are at home, as a word and a number. Hover it to see your prestige too.

On the right are the bell, the day button and **Skip days**. See [Your first week](help:getting-started#the-day-button) for how they work.

## What's on the Office

- **The lead story** is your next main event when a show is close, or last show's winner, or a big news story.
- **Next show** lists what's booked so far. **Open the card** takes you to [Booking](screen:booking).
- **Top stories** are the latest news. **All news** opens the full feed.
- **Standings** ranks the biggest promotions by popularity, with you marked.
- **Money at a glance** shows your cash, this month so far and what wages cost a month.
- **Power rankings** lists your top people by popularity and momentum.

## Needs you today

The side column lists the things worth your attention, each with one button: a show tonight, a contract that is ending, someone who wants to leave, offers waiting for an answer.

When it says _Nothing needs you right now_, book ahead or press **Next day**.

> **Tip:** If your roster is very small, the first item asks you to sign some wrestlers. Shows need people.

## Getting around

The sidebar on the left holds every screen, grouped by **Office**, **World**, **Your promotion**, **Creative**, **Business** and **Help**. A few items are not built yet and say so when you open them.

**Settings** and **Main menu** are at the bottom of the sidebar.
