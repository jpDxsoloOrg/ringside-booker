You run a wrestling promotion. You sign the talent, book the shows, watch them play out and keep the money coming in. This page gets you through your first week.

## Your first week

1. Start on the [Office](help:the-office). It tells you what needs you today.
2. Open [Booking](screen:booking) and fill your next show. **Auto-fill** books the empty slots for you, so you can start there and change what you don't like.
3. Press the day button in the top right to move to the next day. On show night it takes you to the show.
4. Run the show and read the results. They tell you what worked.
5. Look at [Contracts](screen:contracts) for free agents worth signing.

> **Tip:** You don't have to get it all right. A bad show costs you a little, and the next one is only a few days away.

## The day button

The big button in the top right moves the game on. When nothing is waiting it says **Next day**.

When something needs you first, the button names it and takes you there: a show to run, an injury, a contract question or a news alert. Deal with it and the day can move again.

The bell next to it counts what is waiting. Click it to see the list.

## Skipping days

The fast-forward button next to the day button opens **Skip days**. Pick 7, 30 or 90 days, or until the 1st of next month, or type any number from 1 to 3,650.

A skip always stops at your own shows and when a decision arrives, so you never miss anything that needs you. You can stop it yourself too.

## Keys

- `Shift+N` presses the day button.
- `Ctrl+B` folds the sidebar away and back.
- `Escape` closes the sheet on top.

## Saving

The game saves itself as you play. It saves after every day that moves and after the changes you make, and **Main menu** saves before it leaves. There is no save button, and you don't need one.

Each game has its own save slot, plus a backup of the save before. On the title screen, **Continue** opens your latest game and **Load** lists the rest.

> **Note:** This is an early build. A save from an older version may not open in a newer one, so be ready to start fresh.

## Draft start

A new game can **Start with a draft** (Options, Draft). Every wrestler goes into a pool, every promotion starts with the same money and popularity, no TV deal and every title vacant, and the promotions take turns picking before day 1.

- **Active promotions** (Options, Promotions, **Choose…**) picks who takes part, in any game. Search, filter by country or size, or keep **Only the biggest** few. Your promotion is always in. Fewer promotions also play faster.
- The **draft plan** under the options shows the pool, how many sit out and the mix from main eventers down.
- In the **draft room**, the banner says who is on the clock and when you pick next. Sort and filter the pool, by card level too; **Value** and **Fit** each have a quiet Why?. Press **Draft** on your turn, **Sim to my pick** to let the rivals go, or **Auto-pick the rest**. When every pick is made, **Finish the draft**.
- By default each pick signs for one season. With **Negotiate contracts after the draft**, your picks join unsigned: offer each one a contract from the draft room or release them. In a draft game the first show is a week after the start, so they have time to answer. If too few sign, call the show off on Booking.

Until the draft is finished, the day button says **Finish the draft** and takes you back to the room.
